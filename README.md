## About Me

I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at the Academy of Military Medical Sciences, with an undergraduate background in Medical Imaging Technology from Capital Medical University. I began my graduate studies in 2023 and subsequently transitioned from master's to doctoral training.

My research experience spans medical imaging and single-cell multi-omics. My published work includes studies of arterial spin labeling MRI for glioma assessment and the temporal regulation of neural development using single-cell transcriptomic and chromatin accessibility data.

## Research Interests

- **Single-cell multi-omics** — integrating gene expression and chromatin accessibility data to investigate cellular states and biological processes.
- **Gene regulatory networks** — studying transcription factor activity and the temporal organization of gene regulation.
- **Neurodevelopment** — investigating molecular mechanisms underlying neuronal differentiation and maturation.
- **Medical imaging** — exploring MRI-based approaches to brain tumor assessment, including arterial spin labeling and cerebral perfusion imaging.
